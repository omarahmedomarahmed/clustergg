# The pre-relaunch purge — 9 August 2026

Owner-authorised. Every challenge and challenge request built under the old
model was deleted from production before the new one ships, because the two
cannot coexist: the old rows have no `announced_at`, no `baseline_at` on their
entrants, no campaign behind their money and no series behind their weeks, and
every screen built in B90–B91 would have had to special-case them forever.

## What was deleted

| Table | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `challenges` | 6 | 0 |
| `challenge_participants` | 7 | 0 |
| `challenge_events` | 72 | 0 |
| `challenge_requests` | 3 | 0 |

Two of the six were live at the time, with three entrants between them. That was
the explicit instruction and it is the right call before a relaunch: a
competition running under rules that are about to change is a competition whose
result nobody can defend.

## What was NOT touched, and why

**Trophies stayed on profiles.** `user_trophies.challenge_id` is a plain column
with no foreign key, so nothing cascaded: 12 trophies worth $475 are still on
the gamers who won them, still redeemable. Somebody who won something got to
keep it, which is not negotiable — the challenge going away is our
housekeeping, not their loss.

**Money history stayed.** Invoices, the vault ledger and server payouts are
untouched. Deleting what a brand paid because we deleted what they paid for
would make the books unreconcilable.

**Campaign slots were reset, not deleted.** The two running campaigns had slots
pointing at challenges that no longer exist. Every slot is back to `waiting`
with its dates and cover intact, so the campaigns are coherent and can be re-run
under the new model without a brand losing what they bought.

## The undo

A Neon branch `pre-purge-2026-08-09` (`br-restless-unit-assi3cvz`) was created
from the default branch immediately before the deletes. It is a point-in-time
copy of everything above. Delete it once the relaunch has been live long enough
that nobody wants it back.

## What the next deploy does

Nothing about this is in application code — no migration, no startup sweep. It
was a one-off run against production through the Neon MCP, and it is written
down here rather than left as something that happened.
